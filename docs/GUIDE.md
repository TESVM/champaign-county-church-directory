# Guide for directory administrators and church leaders

## Church leaders

**Get access.** Find your church and choose “Manage this listing” (or go to
`/claim`). Use an email on your church’s own domain if you have one; otherwise
explain how we can confirm you (for example, call the church’s public number and
ask for you). You will get an email when a reviewer decides. Access is free.

**Sign in.** Go to “Church sign in”, enter your email, and click the link we
send. It works once and expires in 15 minutes. There is no password. Sessions
last seven days; “Sign out” ends yours immediately.

**Your dashboard** (`/dashboard`):

- *Overview* — profile checklist and activity counts. Counts are actions on this
  website (views, direction and phone taps, link clicks), not attendance.
- *Listing* — name, address, contact details, links, service times, leadership,
  ministries. Saving publishes immediately and marks your listing “Confirmed by
  church” with today’s date. App Store and Google Play links must be real store
  links; links to other sites are rejected with an explanation.
- *Plan your visit* — parking, entrance, accessibility, children, service
  length, dress, languages. Only say what is true today.
- *Photo* — upload (JPEG/PNG/WebP, up to 4 MB) or link a real photo of your
  church, and say who took it and why it may be used. No stock or AI images.
- *Events* (Enhanced) — one-off or weekly events in Central Time; they appear on
  your listing and the county calendar with “Add to calendar” downloads.
- *Community help* — food pantries, meals, clothing, support groups. Reviewed
  by the directory before publishing (and again after edits).
- *Team* (owners) — invite colleagues as editors; remove access at any time.
  Only a directory administrator can add or remove an owner.
- *Billing* (owners) — subscribe to the Enhanced Church Profile ($29/month) and
  “Manage billing” to update the card, see invoices, or cancel.
- *Share & QR* — your listing link and a printable QR code.

## Administrators and reviewers

Roles: **Admin** (everything, including staff roles, complimentary profiles,
disabling accounts), **Reviewer** (claims, submissions, website-review
proposals, community programs, listing status), **Support** (messages and
corrections, read-only elsewhere). Add staff on Admin → Users & staff; they
sign in with an emailed link. The address in `ADMIN_EMAIL` becomes an admin
the first time it signs in.

Daily queues (counts appear in the admin menu):

1. **Claim requests** — the card shows whether the applicant’s email domain
   matches the church website and the church’s *public* phone/email. Approve
   only after confirming authority: matching domain, or a call to the publicly
   listed number. “Ask for more information” and “Decline” email your note to
   the applicant. Approval makes them the owner and emails sign-in instructions.
2. **New churches** — check the church exists; possible duplicates are listed.
   Approval publishes it as “Not yet verified”.
3. **Messages & corrections** — visitor reports go to you and to the church’s
   managers. Fix the listing (“Edit listing”), then “Mark fixed”.
4. **Website review** — weekly automatic checks of churches’ own websites
   (respecting robots.txt; social media is never fetched). Proposals show the
   current value, proposed value, evidence, and source. Nothing changes until you
   click “Apply change”. Be cautious with church-managed listings. “Check the next
   5 websites now” runs a small batch on demand. The table shows every attempt,
   including failures.
5. **Community programs** — publish or reject (with a note).

Per church (Admin → Churches → name):

- **Record check** — after comparing the listing with the church’s website or
  another public source, record the URL; the listing shows “Source checked” and
  the date.
- **Status** — *Closed* (stays reachable with a notice, removed from search),
  *Duplicate* (redirects to the listing you name), *Hidden*, or *Active*.
- **Complimentary enhanced profile** — grant with an end date and a recorded
  reason, or remove.
- **Photo / team / visit info** — open the church’s dashboard pages.
- **History** — every change with who made it.

Other tools: **Export contacts (CSV for Excel)** on the overview or churches
page (name, pastor, address, phone, email, verification and date, source);
**Audit log**; **Email log** (delivery status of every email; sign-in link
contents are never shown).
