# Information to collect for the map and Campus Faith

Nothing below may be guessed or filled in from memory. Each item comes from the
church or organization itself, or from a public page we record as the source.

## From churches

- **Student details**: which of the 15 student tags are true today (Dashboard → Students). Most useful first: near campus or walkable, rides, free meal, evening service, weekday Bible study, young adult ministry, international students welcome.
- **Events for students**: worship nights, college lunches, welcome-week events. Churches tick a "student calendar" category when adding an event (Enhanced profiles).
- **Plan your visit**: wheelchair access, children's program, languages. These power the map filters, and most churches have not filled them in yet.
- **Address fixes for the map**: two addresses did not match the Census geocoder:
  - Temple Baptist Church (1100 Broadmoor Dr, Champaign)
  - New Covenant Fellowship (124 W White St, listed as Urbana; please confirm the city)
- **Sheriff Temple AOH**: which student tags apply, if any. It shows as "Featured" today, but it is not listed as student-friendly until the church confirms something.

## From UIUC campus ministries

- Organization name, campuses, affiliation, and partner church
- Description, who is welcome, and what a first visit is like
- Meeting day, time, and location (building and room), with types of gathering
- Website, Instagram, Facebook, and sign-up links; a logo they own
- A leader's name, role, and email (kept private unless they choose to publish), plus their consent to publish
- Upcoming events: date, time, location, category, and sign-up link

## From Parkland College organizations

- The same fields as UIUC
- Whether they meet on campus or at a partner church
- Whether they welcome students from other schools

## From student leaders

- Permission before any student's name or contact appears publicly
- Which officer should receive review emails, and who takes over each year
- Semester start and welcome-week dates, so events go up early
